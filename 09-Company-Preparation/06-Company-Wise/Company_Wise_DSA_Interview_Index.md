Do not prepare all groups simultaneously. Select the real target employers for your next application cycle.

## 3. Company workflow

1. Open the company folder in the Liquidslr repository.
2. Review recent 30/60/90-day data where available, then recurring all-time fundamentals.
3. Record LeetCode ID and canonical title.
4. Map it to the DSA Pattern Master Index.
5. Add company tag to the canonical problem; do not duplicate the solution.
6. Record whether it was independent, hinted or editorial-assisted.
7. Before an interview, combine a small sample of recent tagged questions with unseen questions from the same patterns.

A tag does not guarantee that the company will ask that question. It may not identify round, level, location or date accurately.

## 5. Completion gate

A company set is ready when its questions map to learned patterns, duplicates are removed, recent tagged problems are sampled (not memorised), you can solve representative questions without labels, and you can explain solutions under interview conditions.
